# The Artist’s Way — creative practice

Documentation updated: 2026-11-09.

My TouchDesigner practice combines The Artist’s Way framework, paper notes, linked Zettelkasten notes, and small experiments. The archived plan set out an 84-day / 12-week study window from May 29 to August 20, 2025. The snapshot records day 29 / week 5 on June 26, 2025; it is a historical plan, not a claim that all twelve weeks were completed.

1. Watch a lesson and notice what invites an experiment.
2. Take paper notes and follow along in TouchDesigner.
3. Make a small variation and record what changes.
4. Break the notes into linked, reusable ideas.
5. Save the patch, images, and next questions.
